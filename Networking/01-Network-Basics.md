# 🌐 Network Basics

## What is a Network?

A network is a collection of interconnected devices that communicate and share resources.

---

## Why Do We Need Networks?

- Communication
- Resource Sharing
- Internet Access
- Data Exchange

---

## Networking vs Internet

### Networking
Networking is the process of connecting devices together for communication and resource sharing.

### Internet
The Internet is a global network of interconnected networks.

---

## Types of Networks

### PAN (Personal Area Network)
A small network around an individual.

**Examples:**
- Bluetooth Earbuds
- Smart Watch

### LAN (Local Area Network)
A network within a limited area.

**Examples:**
- Home WiFi
- School Computer Lab

### MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
A network that covers a city.

**Examples:**
- City-wide ISP Network

### WAN (Wide Area Network)
A network spanning large geographical areas.

**Examples:**
- The Internet
- Corporate Networks

---

## Real World Examples

- Home WiFi
- Office Network
- School Network
- Mobile Network

---

## Summary

A network allows devices to communicate and share resources. Networks can range from small personal networks to the global Internet.
